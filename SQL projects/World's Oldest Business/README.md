# Business Analysis Project

## Overview

In this project, I explored datasets from DataCamp to analyze business trends across different countries and categories. My main goal was to understand the dynamics of businesses founded in recent years and how they are distributed globally.

## Datasets Used

### Businesses and New Businesses
- **business**: Name of the business (varchar)
- **year_founded**: Year the business was founded (int)
- **category_code**: Code for the business category (varchar)
- **country_code**: ISO 3166-1 three-letter country code (char)

### Countries
- **country_code**: ISO 3166-1 three-letter country code (varchar)
- **country**: Name of the country (varchar)
- **continent**: Name of the continent where the country is located (varchar)

### Categories
- **category_code**: Code for the business category (varchar)
- **category**: Description of the business category (varchar)

## Business Questions Addressed

1. **Top Countries**: Identified the top 5 countries with the highest number of businesses founded in the last 5 years.
2. **Business Categories**: Analyzed which business categories have the highest percentage of new businesses established in the last 10 years.
3. **Major Categories**: Investigated countries that have businesses across all major categories (the top 5 by number).
4. **Year Distribution**: Examined how business founding years are distributed across different continents.
5. **Average Years Since Founding**: Found which business categories have the highest average number of years since they were founded, across all countries.

## Methodology

- **Data Cleaning**: Ensured the datasets were clean and free of duplicates or missing values.
- **Data Analysis**: Utilized SQL queries and Python for data manipulation and analysis.

## Key Findings

The analysis provided valuable insights into the growth of businesses in various countries and categories, informing strategic decisions for investors and entrepreneurs. Understanding the distribution of founding years also sheds light on emerging markets and sectors.

## Conclusion

This project showcases my ability to work with real-world datasets and demonstrates my analytical skills in understanding business trends. The insights gained can be valuable for anyone looking to invest or expand in specific markets.
