## 📈 Key Insights

### ☕ Best-Selling Coffee Types
  - **Excelsa & Liberica (Light Roast)** and **Aberica (Medium Roast)** emerged as customer favorites.

### 🌎 Country-Specific Observations
- **United States** leads overall sales volume, followed closely by **Ireland**.
- Irish buyers show a preference for **Liberica Light** and **Robusta Dark**.

### 📈 Yearly Performance Highlights:
        - 2021 was the highest revenue year: $13,766
        - 2022 had the lowest sales: $7,063 – likely due to data ending in August
-**📊 Trend Observations:**
       - Arabica and Liberica sales peaked in 2021.
       - Excelsa performed consistently well across all years.
       - Robusta maintained the lowest but relatively stable performance.

### 📅 Seasonal Sales Trends Insights
    -🔺**Peak Months (High Sales):**  October – Highest overall ($5,285)
                                       April – Strong spring sales ($5,225)
                                       March and June – Also consistently high
     -🔻 **Low Months (Low Sales):**  May and August – Tend to be the weakest
                                      January – Also a slow start after holidays
  
  ###  📅Seasonal Patterns:     Q1 (Jan–Mar): Gradual increase from post-holiday low to strong March
                                Q2 (Apr–Jun): Consistently high-performing quarter
                                Q3 (Jul–Sep): More variable — July and August dip, September rebounds
                                Q4 (Oct–Dec): Strong finish, with October as the peak

### 💳 Loyalty Impact
- Customers using loyalty cards contributed **4% less revenue** on average than non-loyalty customers.
